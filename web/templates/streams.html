An e-commerce platform needs a durable record of all order events. Unlike core NATS where events vanish after delivery, JetStream stores them in a stream so services can replay historical events after a crash, audit what happened, or let new services catch up on past events they missed.

Try it: Create the ORDERS stream, publish some order events, then replay them. Try purging and re-publishing. Watch the sequence numbers and stream stats update in real time.
